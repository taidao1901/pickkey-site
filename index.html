Pick Key
Review videos for @pickkeyreal

Product review videos, published to TikTok the official way.

Pick Key is the production and publishing tool behind the TikTok channel @pickkeyreal. It turns buyer reviews into short vertical videos, lets the channel owner approve each one, and publishes the approved video to TikTok through the official Content Posting API.

Not a public service. Pick Key runs on the channel owner's own computer and only ever publishes to the TikTok account that authorised it. There is no sign-up, no third-party accounts, and nothing is posted automatically: every video is reviewed and approved before it is sent to TikTok.

What it does

  1. Collect — reads product reviews and measurements for one product.
  2. Build — writes the script, records the voice-over and renders a 9:16 video.
  3. Review — the owner watches the finished video and the caption in a local review page and approves this exact video.
  4. Publish — the tool requests the creator's privacy options, uploads the MP4 in chunks and calls the Content Posting API with the approved caption; it then polls the publish status and records the result.

TikTok permissions used

  • user.info.basic — show which TikTok account the post will go to (Login Kit consent screen).
  • video.publish — direct-post an approved video to that account.
  • video.upload — deliver a draft to that account's TikTok inbox for editing in the app.

Pick Key does not read messages, analytics or other people's data, and does not automate likes, comments or follows.

How publishing works

The tool uses POST /v2/post/publish/creator_info/query/ to read the account's available privacy levels, POST /v2/post/publish/video/init/ to start the upload, a chunked PUT to the returned upload URL (5–64 MB per chunk, as in TikTok's Media Transfer Guide), and POST /v2/post/publish/status/fetch/ to follow the post through processing and moderation.
