Pick Key — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 November 2026 · Last updated: 22 November 2026

Pick Key ("the App") is a private tool used by the owner of the TikTok channel @pickkeyreal to produce and publish that channel's own review videos. This policy explains the small amount of data the App touches. It applies to the App only; TikTok's own privacy policy governs the TikTok platform.

1. Data the App accesses through TikTok

DataWhy
OAuth access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok after the channel owner consents To call the Content Posting API on the owner's behalf
Basic profile identifiers returned by the API (open_id, username, nickname) To show which TikTok account a post would be published to
Publishing status of posts the App created (processing, published, failed, post id) To report the outcome and to avoid publishing the same video twice

The App does not read the account's private messages, does not read other users' data, does not collect viewer analytics, and does not access advertising or payment data.

2. Data the App stores and where

  • Tokens are stored in a local configuration file on the operator's own computer, readable only by the operating-system user that runs the App (file mode 0600).
  • Publishing state (which video was sent, its publish id and result) is stored locally in the operator's own database.
  • Video files are read from local disk and sent to TikTok's upload endpoint. The App does not keep a copy in any cloud storage of its own.

No data is sold, rented or shared with third parties. The only external service involved is TikTok itself, and only for the calls described above.

3. Legal basis and retention

The channel owner consents to the access on TikTok's OAuth screen; withdrawing consent is possible at any time. Tokens are kept only while the integration is in use — the TikTok refresh token expires after 180 days of inactivity, and deleting the local configuration file removes all stored credentials immediately.

4. Your choices

  • Revoke the App's access at any time in TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security and login → Manage app permissions (or the equivalent screen on your device).
  • Ask for deletion of anything the App stored by writing to the address below.

5. Children

The App is not directed to children and is not offered to the public. It is used only by the channel owner, who meets TikTok's minimum age requirement.

6. Changes

This policy may be updated when the App's behaviour changes; the effective date above always reflects the current version.

7. Contact

Privacy questions or deletion requests: pickkeyreal@gmail.com.
