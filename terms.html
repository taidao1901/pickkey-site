The App is not offered, sold, or licensed to the public, and it does not post content to any account other than the one that authorised it.

1. Who may use the App

The App is operated by the Pick Key team for its own channel. Access is limited to the TikTok account holder who explicitly authorises the App through TikTok's standard OAuth consent screen. No third-party user accounts are created inside the App.

2. What the App does

  • Builds short vertical video reviews from product information and buyer reviews.
  • Uploads a finished video file to TikTok through the TikTok Content Posting API, either as a draft in the creator's inbox or as a direct post, only after the channel owner approves that specific video.
  • Reads the publishing status of the posts it created (e.g. processing, published, failed).
